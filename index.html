WHAT IS CURBS?

Curbs is a street game for two players. Stand on opposite sides of the road and take turns throwing a ball at the curb across from you. Hit the edge and bounce it back to score.

A hit from your curb scores 10 points and moves you halfway across. From there, each hit is worth 5 points. Keep throwing until you miss, then it’s your rival’s turn. First to 100 wins!

Share a screen with a friend or take on the CPU.

HOW TO PLAY

  1. Start, power, aim. Tap your side (Player 1 left, Player 2 right) or press Q for Player 1 and P for Player 2. Once to start the power meter, again to lock power, and once more to lock aim and throw. Space also works on your turn. Against the CPU, tap anywhere.
  2. Watch the needles. Green on both meters gets you a hit. Yellow edges give you a 50/50 chance. From the curb, hit gold on both for Double Trouble, a bounce off both curbs worth 30 points.
  3. Street distractions. Cars pause play and the dog makes your aim wobble. You also get two NOODLES! per match. Hit your button (or X for Player 1, N for Player 2) while your rival sets up a throw to shake their aim.
  4. Pinky’s 99 race. Wait for NOM!, then tap your side or mash Q / P to eat. First to finish wins. During a match, the winner earns a 99 BOOST with bigger gold zones on their next throw from the curb.

THE STREET RULES

  1. Throw the ball at the far curb. First to 100 wins.
  2. Timing: tap your half of the screen (left is Player 1, right is Player 2) or press Q / P. Once to start, once to lock power, once to lock aim. Space works for whoever’s up.
  3. Green on both meters is a sure curb. Yellow edges are a 50:50. Anything else misses.
  4. Curb hit: +10, then you move to halfway. Halfway hits are +5 and you keep going until you miss. The zones are smaller there.
  5. Double Trouble: +30. From the curb, find the thin gold power zone with near-perfect aim and the ball comes all the way back to your own curb.
  6. Drag to throw: pull back from your player, move sideways to aim, let go.
  7. NOODLES! Two per game. While your rival sets power or aim, press X (Player 1), N (Player 2) or your NOODLES! button. Their aim goes wobbly.
  8. Cars stop play. Fluffy dogs are distracting. And if you hear Pinky’s van, the first to finish their 99 gets a 99 BOOST: next throw from the curb with a huge gold zone.
